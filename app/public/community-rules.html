Community rules

Version 2026-11-03 · Sangam is run by Turtletech Labs

Sangam is a community for Indians abroad and their families back home. People use it to find help, answers and friends, and some are new, far from home and trusting. These rules keep it safe. By using Sangam you agree to follow them.

The short version

  • Be real. Be kind. Never ask anyone for money, codes or private details.
  • No scams, fraud or phishing.
  • No obscene, sexual, violent or hateful content.
  • Don't share other people's private information.
  • If something feels wrong, press Report.

What is not allowed

Scams, fraud and phishing

You must not post, send or link to anything meant to cheat people or steal from them. That includes:

  • Asking for a deposit, fee or payment before someone has seen a home, started a job or received what you offer.
  • Fake jobs, fake visa or sponsorship offers, or "guaranteed" immigration help in return for money.
  • Pretending to be a bank, a government body, the police, an employer, a landlord, or Sangam.
  • Asking for passwords, one-time codes (OTPs), card or bank details, passport or ID numbers.
  • Links to look-alike websites, or links meant to collect someone's details.
  • Investment, crypto, "double your money", chain-referral or pyramid schemes.
  • Gift-card requests, fake prizes, fake charity appeals and fake emergencies ("my relative is in hospital, send money now").
  • Selling or sharing fake documents, accounts or reviews.

Obscene and sexual content

  • No nudity, pornography or sexually explicit writing, jokes or links.
  • No sexual solicitation, escort or "companionship" offers.
  • Any sexual content involving a child is banned with no exceptions. We report it to the police.

Harassment, hate and violence

  • No threats, bullying, stalking or repeated unwanted contact.
  • No hate or abuse aimed at people because of their religion, caste, race, nationality, gender, sexuality, disability or the region they come from.
  • No content that encourages violence, self-harm or crime.

Privacy of others

  • Don't post another person's phone number, address, photos, documents or messages without their clear permission.
  • Don't name and shame private individuals. If someone has wronged you, report it to us and to the proper authorities.
  • Don't screenshot and share private chats.

Illegal and harmful goods and services

  • Drugs, weapons, fake or stolen documents, stolen goods.
  • Selling visas, sponsorship, "certificates of sponsorship" or jobs for money.
  • Unlicensed money transfer, trafficking, or helping someone to enter or stay in a country illegally.
  • Medical advice or "cures" that could put someone at risk.

Spam, fake accounts and misuse

  • No repeated posts, mass messages, adverts or affiliate links.
  • One account per person, in your real name or the name you are known by. No impersonating anyone.
  • No fake emergency alerts, and no false reports.
  • Don't try to break the app, get around its limits, or collect other members' details.

Jobs, referrals and housing

These need extra care because they are where scams are most common. They can only be posted by members at level 3.

  • Be real: name the employer or landlord, and describe the actual role or home.
  • Never charge applicants or tenants a fee to apply, to "hold" a place, or to be considered.
  • Never take a deposit before the person has viewed the home and seen a written agreement.

What you can post

Sangam currently supports text posts, replies and messages. If we add photos or files, you may only share ones you made or have the right to share, and the rules above apply to them too.

You keep ownership of what you write. By posting, you give us permission to show it to other members of Sangam and to store it so the service works. You are responsible for what you post.

Staying safe when you meet people

  • Meet in a public place, and tell someone where you are going.
  • Never send money to someone you haven't met. Never pay in advance for a home or a job.
  • Be careful with anyone who rushes you, wants to move to another app, or asks for codes or payments.
  • Sangam does not check every member. Trust levels and badges (below) tell you how someone signed up. They are not a guarantee, and they are not our endorsement of what a member says or does.

Trust levels and badges

  • Level 1: signed in with an email address. Can read and use the safety tools.
  • Level 2: signed in with a work or university email, or joined with an invite code. Can post, reply and send chat requests.
  • Level 3: a member whose identity we have checked, or who our team knows. Can also post jobs, referrals and housing. During the beta, ID checks are being introduced, and some level 3 members have been given the level by our team because we know them.
  • Expert badge: given by our team to members with relevant professional experience. It is not legal, tax, medical or immigration advice.

What happens if you break the rules

We may remove content, warn you, limit what you can do, suspend or delete your account, or tell the police or another authority if the law or someone's safety requires it. We decide based on how serious the behaviour is and what the person has done before. If you think we got it wrong, email us and we will look again.

Reporting

Use Report on any post, reply, message or profile. Reports are private and the person you report is not told who you are. We read every report and deal with the most serious ones first.

Nobody should wait for us in an emergency. If someone is in danger, call your local emergency number (112 or 999 in Ireland, 999 in the UK). Sangam is not an emergency service.

You can also block anyone from their profile. Blocking hides each of you from the other and stops messages.
