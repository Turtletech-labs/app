Privacy policy

Version 2026-11-03 · This is the policy for the Sangam beta

Who we are

Sangam is run by Turtletech Labs ("we", "us"). We decide how your personal data is used, which makes us the "controller" under data protection law. You can reach us about anything in this policy at turtletech.labs@gmail.com.

Where the law requires it, we will appoint a representative in the EU and the UK before Sangam is opened to the public, and list their details here.

The short version

  • We collect only what we need to run the community: your email, the profile you fill in, what you post and send, and the requests you make.
  • We never sell your data and we show no ads. There are no advertising or analytics trackers in Sangam.
  • Your trusted contacts and emergency card stay on your phone. We never receive them.
  • Your location is read once, only when you press SOS, and goes straight from your phone to the people you chose. It does not come to us.
  • Other members can see your profile and posts. They never see your email address.
  • You can delete your account yourself, any time. It removes your data from our live systems straight away.

Some things you write may be sensitive (for example, a service request about a parent's health). We use anything like that only to carry out your request and run Sangam. By choosing to put it in, you agree to us handling it for that purpose. Please don't include more than you need to.

We don't make decisions about you by automated means that have legal or similar effects. If we ever use automated tools to screen posts for scams or abuse, we will update this policy first and a person will review any decision that affects you.

What stays on your phone

These are saved only in your device's storage (for a web browser, that browser's local storage), not on our servers:

  • Trusted contacts: names, phone numbers, relationship.
  • Emergency card: blood group, allergies, medications, conditions, insurance and notes.
  • Your sign-in token, which keeps you signed in.

When you press SOS, Sangam asks your phone for its location once, builds a message, and opens your phone's call and messaging apps. Sangam does not track your location in the background, does not store it, and does not send it to us. Deleting your account also clears this data from the device you delete it on.

Who can see your information

  • Other people who sign in to Sangam can see your name, city, work, college, home state, bio, trust level and badges, and what you post and reply. Your email address and phone number are never shown to them.
  • Private chats can be read only by the two people in them. Messages are stored on our servers so that they work across your devices, and they are not end-to-end encrypted. We don't read chats. A small number of our team can technically access the database, and would look at a message only if it was reported, to investigate abuse or a safety concern, or if the law requires it.
  • Reports are private. The person you report is not told who you are.
  • Our service providers act on our instructions and handle data only to provide their service:
    • Supabase (database and sign-in), with our data stored in Frankfurt, Germany;
    • Cloudflare (delivers the website and app);
    • Google (sends the sign-in code emails for us).
  • Service partners. If a service you request needs a partner (for example a regulated tax adviser), we will tell you and share only what is needed, and only with your agreement.
  • Authorities. We may share information where the law requires it or where it is needed to protect someone from serious harm.
  • If Sangam is ever sold or merged, your data would move with it under the same protections, and we would tell you first.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

Where your data goes

Our database is hosted in the EU (Frankfurt). Our team works from India, so some access to the data happens from outside the EU and UK. We keep that access to a small number of people, protect it with strong sign-in, and will put in place the safeguards the law requires. Our providers may also process data in other countries, including the US. They do so under their data protection terms, which include safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • see the data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • have wrong data corrected (you can edit your profile in the app);
  • have your data deleted (use Delete account in your Profile, or email us);
  • object to us using your data based on our legitimate interests, or ask us to restrict it;
  • move your data to another service;
  • withdraw consent at any time where we rely on it. This doesn't affect what we did before.

Email us to use any of these. We reply within one month. We may ask you to prove who you are first.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to your data protection authority. In Ireland that is the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie). In the UK it is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Keeping your data safe

  • Everything travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Access rules in our database stop members reading each other's private data, and stop members changing things they shouldn't (such as their own trust level). These rules are tested automatically every time we change the code.
  • There are no passwords to steal: you sign in with a one-time code sent to your email.
  • We limit how fast accounts can post, message and guess codes, to slow down abuse.
  • Our team's access is limited to what each person needs.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your data at risk, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Children

Sangam is for adults aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If we learn that someone under 18 has joined, we will delete the account.

Cookies and similar storage

We don't use advertising, tracking or analytics cookies. The only things stored on your device are the ones needed for Sangam to work: your sign-in token and the on-device data described above. You can clear them by signing out, deleting your account, or clearing your browser's site data.

Safety features

Sangam is not an emergency service. In an emergency, call your local emergency number (112 or 999 in Ireland, 999 in the UK).

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy as Sangam grows. If a change matters to you, we will tell you in the app and ask you to accept the new version. Each version has a date at the top.
